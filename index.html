Biography / 02

Two countries.
One evolving path.

I’m Haykal Fikri Ramadhan, a web designer and Informatics graduate from Universitas Siliwangi. My story crosses Indonesia and England: I moved to England in 2012, studied at Archbishop Holgate’s School in York, then returned to Indonesia in 2016 to continue secondary school before moving into technology and digital design.

That experience shaped how I work today—adaptable, curious, and focused on making complex ideas feel clear.
