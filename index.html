Who I am

Designing around people, not only appearances.

I’m Nabila Azmi, an interior designer with experience in freelance projects and professional design studios. My work covers concept development, space planning, technical drawings, and 3D visualization.

For me, every space tells a story. Great design begins by understanding people’s lifestyles, needs, emotions, and the way a space must perform every day.

Concept Development Design direction, mood, materials, and spatial identity.
Space Planning Efficient layouts, circulation, zoning, and user flow.
Technical Drawings Clear documentation for design coordination and execution.
3D Visualization Detailed visual studies to communicate atmosphere and intent.
