Data Science · Undergraduate Research

Sales Forecasting Model

My undergraduate final project at Universitas Siliwangi used historical UMKM sales data to build a forecasting model. I prepared and visualised the data to explore sales patterns, then applied polynomial regression to generate sales predictions. The analysis was developed with Python and scikit-learn in Google Colab, with the methodology and findings documented in my thesis.
